Tourist - Help

Help

How each screen works, with a picture and the usual order of use. Everything is in the app and needs no network.

Screens

  • TouristScreenThe start screen: pick the program you need.
  • Tourist dashboardScreenSpeed, compass, coordinates and the trip: one big card and a grid of tiles.
  • CounterScreenA tally counter: add, subtract, set a starting number, keep a history.
  • LightsScreenWater, front and camera flashlights, and how to leave each.
  • SOS emergency signalScreenThe emergency signal: it starts when the screen opens and ends with the stop button.
  • StopwatchScreenTiming one, two or four people at once, with laps.
  • SettingsScreenChoose theme, language and units.

Windows

  • Stopwatch settingsWindowParticipants, music and the volume keys.
  • Stopwatch resultWindowSave the times as a text file or share them.
