Stopwatch

The stopwatch times one, two or four people side by side, each with their own time and laps. It starts counting for everyone as soon as the screen opens and keeps counting while the phone is locked or another app is in front, but the times are gone once you leave the screen with Back. It is meant for everyday timing, not as an official timing device.

In this order

  1. If you time more than one person, open the settings 6 first and pick two or four participants, so that everyone has an area before the start.
  2. The count begins by itself when the screen opens. To start over from zero, press 4, then start each participant in their area 1, or everyone at once with 3 when there are several.
  3. Press the first button in a participant's area 1 to mark a lap without stopping the count.
  4. Press the second button when a participant finishes; to stop everyone together press 3, which now reads Stop all.
  5. Press 5 to turn the times into text you can save or share.
  6. Press 4 once the run is over and saved. The times are not kept: leaving the screen with Back clears them.

The elements

1ParticipantsA separate area for each person you time.

Each participant has an area with a large time (minutes, seconds and hundredths, with hours after the first hour) and a list of laps, newest on top. The first button reads Start while the participant is stopped and Lap while it counts; the second reads Stop while it counts and Reset when stopped.

Why
Every participant counts on their own, so starting, lapping or stopping one never touches another.
Next
Lap adds a line with the time shown at that moment and the count goes on; Start after a stop carries on from the stopped time.
Undo
After Stop you can press Start again, but Reset clears that participant's time and laps for good, with no confirmation.
Shortcut
On a connected keyboard: 1-4 select a participant, Space or Enter starts or laps, S stops, R clears the time (also while it counts). With one participant the keys act on it at once; with several, select the one you need first.
Tip

Tapping the large time works like the first button. With Volume keys start and stop turned on in the settings, volume up starts or laps and volume down stops - for the first participant, or for the one you selected with a keyboard.

2Music volumeSets the volume the music plays at.

The slider above the buttons sets the phone's media volume, which is the level the music plays at. It is shown whether or not music is turned on.

Why
While Volume keys start and stop is on, the volume keys run the stopwatch instead of changing the sound, so this slider is the way to change the volume.
Next
The new level applies at once.
Undo
Yes, move the slider back.
3Start allStarts or stops every participant together.

While every participant is stopped it reads Start all and starts them all together; while at least one participant is counting it reads Stop all and stops them all together. It is shown when there are two or four participants.

Why
Use it for a common start or a common finish: everyone starts or stops at the same instant.
Next
Stopped participants keep their times and laps, and starting again carries on from them.
Undo
It clears nothing: Stop all keeps every time, and the next start carries on from it.
4Reset allStops everyone and clears all times and laps.

Stops every participant and sets the time and the lap list of each to zero, including participants hidden by a smaller participant count.

Why
Use it to prepare a new run, or once the result has been saved or shared.
Next
Every area shows zero and waits for Start.
Undo
No. The times and laps are lost for good and there is no confirmation, so save or share the result first.
5ResultTurns the times into text to save or share.

Opens the Stopwatch result window with the times and laps of the participants on screen. If nothing has been measured, a short message says Nothing has been measured yet instead.

Why
Use it to keep a record of a run or to pass the times on.
Next
The window shows the times as they were when you pressed the button; the stopwatch behind it keeps counting if it was counting.
Undo
Opening it changes nothing; Cancel returns to the stopwatch.
6Stopwatch settingsParticipants, music and volume keys.

The gear button opens the Stopwatch settings window, where you choose how many people you time, whether music plays and what the volume keys do.

Why
Use it before a run to set the screen up for your group, or to add music.
Next
Choices take effect when you press Apply; the stopwatch keeps counting and no time is cleared.
Undo
Opening it changes nothing; Cancel closes the window without saving.
7Help (F1)Opens this page.

A question mark in the top-right corner of the screen opens this page.

Why
It explains the screen with a picture, without leaving the app and without a network.
Next
The stopwatch keeps counting while help is open; Back returns to it.
Shortcut
F1 on a connected keyboard does the same.
