SOS emergency signal

The signal starts as soon as this screen opens: a siren and a flashing light repeat the Morse SOS pattern. To end it, press Stop signal or leave the screen with Back. It sounds and blinks, and a paired watch signals too; it sends no SMS, makes no call and contacts no one.

In this order

  1. Open the screen only when you want the signal. It starts at once, in the mode you used last (Sound and light the first time), and a paired watch is asked to signal too.
  2. Pick another mode with 1 if you need one: the running signal changes at once and the choice is kept for next time.
  3. You can leave the screen or lock the phone: the signal goes on in the background. If notifications are allowed for the app, its notification has its own Stop signal button.
  4. To end the signal press 2 or leave the screen with Back: the phone stops, a paired watch is asked to stop too, and the screen closes.
  5. If you do not stop it, the phone ends its own signal after 30 minutes to spare the battery and the flash; the count starts again whenever the signal is started or its mode changes. Open the screen again to signal again.
  6. Press 3 to read this page: the signal goes on while you read.

The elements

1Signal modeWhat the signal uses: sound, light or both.

Three buttons, one chosen at a time: Sound and light runs the siren and the light together, Sound only the siren alone, Light only the light alone - the camera flash and the flashing screen.

Why
Pick Light only where a siren would draw the wrong attention, and Sound only to keep the flash and the flashing screen off.
Next
The running signal changes at once, the choice is kept for next time, and a paired watch is asked to follow.
Undo
Tap another button to change it back.
Tip

The siren uses the alarm volume, which the phone raises to the maximum while the signal runs and puts back when it stops. On a phone without a flash the flashing screen is the light.

2Stop signalEnds the signal on the phone and the watch.

A large button that stops the siren and the light, asks a paired watch to stop too, and closes the screen.

Why
It is larger than the other buttons, so that a cold or wet hand can find it.
Next
You are back where you came from, and the alarm volume is set back to what it was.
Undo
A stopped signal does not resume; open the screen again to start a new one.
Shortcut
On a keyboard or D-pad the button has the focus when the screen opens; Enter presses it.
Tip

Back does the same as this button. Going to the home screen or locking the phone does not: the signal goes on, and with notifications allowed for the app its notification has the same Stop signal button. Stopping on the watch stops the phone's signal too. A signal started on the watch asks the phone to signal as well; if Android refuses to start it in the background, the notification SOS started on the watch offers it - tap it.

3Help (F1)Opens this page.

The question mark in the top-right corner opens the page for this screen over the running signal.

Why
It explains the screen, with a picture, without leaving the app and without a network.
Next
The signal goes on while you read; Back closes the page and returns to the screen.
Shortcut
F1 on a connected keyboard does the same.
