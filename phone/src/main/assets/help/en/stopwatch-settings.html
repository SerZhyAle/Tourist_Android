Stopwatch settings

This window opens from the stopwatch screen with the gear button (Stopwatch settings). Choose how many people you time, whether music plays and what the volume keys do; Apply keeps the choices, and Cancel or Back returns to the stopwatch without changes.

In this order

  1. Pick the number of participants 1: 1, 2 or 4. Do it first, because it decides how the stopwatch screen is divided.
  2. Turn on 2 if you want a track during the run, then open 3 and tap a track; choosing a track turns the music on by itself.
  3. Decide what the volume keys do 4: leave it on to start, lap and stop with the volume buttons, or turn it off to keep them for the volume.
  4. Press Apply to keep the choices. Cancel, Back or Esc closes the window and leaves everything as it was.

The elements

1ParticipantsHow many people are timed: 1, 2 or 4.

A drop-down list with three choices: 1, 2 or 4. The stopwatch screen is divided between that many participants, each with their own time and laps.

Why
Pick as many participants as you have people to time; with one, the whole screen shows a single time.
Next
After Apply the stopwatch screen shows that many areas, and with two or four a button for all participants at once appears.
Undo
Yes, choose another number. Lowering it only hides participants, and their times come back when you raise it again.
Tip

A hidden participant is not stopped: one that was counting keeps counting out of sight, and only the participants on screen go into the result.

2Play music while it runsPlays a track while a measurement runs.

When on, the chosen track plays on a loop for as long as at least one participant is counting, and falls silent when all are stopped.

Why
Turn it on for a run you want to do to music, off for a silent one.
Next
Pick the track in the Track row; without one the stopwatch stays silent.
Undo
Yes, turn it off again.
Tip

The music plays only while the stopwatch screen is in front: it stops when you switch to another app or lock the phone, and starts again when you come back while a measurement is running. A change made during a run is heard from the next start.

3TrackChooses the music from the phone.

Opens the Choose music list of the audio files on the phone: the title, with the artist and the length under it when known; tap a track to choose it. The row shows the chosen track, or Not chosen.

Why
Choose the music you want to hear during a run.
Next
Tapping a track returns here, shows its name in the row and turns Play music while it runs on; the choice is kept when you press Apply.
Undo
Yes, choose another track or turn the music off; Back closes the list without choosing.
Tip

The list asks for access to your audio files, and it can show tracks only once you allow it. If it says that no music was found, copy audio files to the phone and open the list again.

4Volume keys start and stopLets the volume buttons run the stopwatch.

On by default. While the stopwatch is open, volume up starts or laps and volume down stops; turned off, the buttons go back to the system and change the volume as usual.

Why
Use it when pressing a button is easier than touching the screen; turn it off if you would rather keep the buttons for the volume.
Next
With several participants the buttons act on the first one, or on the one you selected with a keyboard. While this is on, the music volume is set with the slider on the stopwatch screen.
Undo
Yes, turn it off and press Apply; the buttons return to the system.
5Help (F1)Opens this page.

The question mark in the title of the window opens this page.

Why
It explains the window with a picture, without leaving the app and without a network.
Next
Back closes help and returns to this window.
Shortcut
F1 on a connected keyboard does the same.
