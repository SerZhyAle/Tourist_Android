Screen help

Tourist

The start screen lists the programs of the kit. Tap the one you need; each opens on its own screen and Back returns here.

In this order

  1. Pick the program you need from the four rows 1 2 3 4.
  2. Use Back on any program to return to this screen.
  3. Press the question mark 6 on any screen to read how that screen works.

The elements

1Tourist dashboardSpeed, altitude, compass, coordinates and the trip.

Opens the dashboard: one large tile and a grid of smaller ones with the readings of your phone and, when it is paired, of your watch.

Why
It is the screen to keep open on the trail: where you are, which way you face and how far you have come.
Next
The dashboard asks for location access the first time and shows what it can read without it.
Undo
Opening it changes nothing; Back returns here.
2LightsWater, front and camera flashlights.

Opens the lights: the water flashlight that wet touches cannot switch off, the front flashlight that turns the screen into a lamp, and the camera flashlight.

Why
Pick the light that fits the moment: the water flashlight for rain and rivers, the front one for reading a map, the camera one for the brightest beam.
Next
Each light opens on its own screen; the Lights page explains how to leave every one of them.
Undo
Opening the list switches no light on.
3SOS emergency signalThe emergency signal.

Opens the SOS screen. The signal starts as soon as the screen opens: the siren and the strobe repeat the Morse SOS pattern, and a paired watch signals too.

Why
Use it when you need to be noticed. The signal sounds and blinks; it does not call anyone and sends no message.
Next
The signal keeps going until you press Stop signal or leave the screen with Back.
Undo
Stop signal ends it at once.
4StopwatchA stopwatch for up to several people at once.

Opens the stopwatch, which times several participants side by side, with laps, results and optional music.

Why
It suits a race, a relay or any case where more than one person needs a time.
Next
The count starts for everyone as soon as the screen opens; each participant has their own lap and stop buttons, and the group can be stopped or reset together.
Undo
Leaving the screen with Back clears the times; Reset all starts over at once.
5SettingsApplication settings.

Opens theme, language, units, screen-awake settings and About. Changes apply immediately.

6Help (F1)Opens this page.

Every screen of the app has this button in its top-right corner, beside what is already there.

Why
It explains the screen you are looking at, with a picture, without leaving the app and without a network.
Shortcut
F1 on a connected keyboard does the same.
