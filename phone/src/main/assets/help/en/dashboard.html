Tourist dashboard

The dashboard shows what your phone reads on the trail: one reading large on the big card, the others as tiles below it. These are readings of your phone's sensors, not an exact instrument. The buttons at the bottom open a map, share your position, reset the trip and open SOS.

In this order

  1. If the card 9 is at the top, press Allow (or Open settings) in it: without the access the dashboard cannot read your position or steps.
  2. Read the big card 1: it shows one reading in large type, Speed at first.
  3. Look through the grid 3 and tap the tile you want to read large: it takes the place of the big card.
  4. While the big card 1 shows Coordinates, tapping it copies the position; on Counter it opens the counter.
  5. The Map button 4 shows you in a maps app, the Share button 5 passes the position on as text; both leave the app.
  6. At the start of a new leg press the Reset Trip button 6, or the reset button 2 on the big card, so the trip counts from zero.
  7. Press the SOS signal button 7 when you need to be noticed; press 8 to read this page again.

The elements

1The big cardThe tile you picked, in large type.

Shows one tile large, Speed at first. On Compass the card draws a compass rose that turns as you turn the phone, with the heading in degrees, the cardinal direction and a line with the sensor's accuracy and Magnetic north; once the phone has a position, the line adds the true heading. The Magnetic field tile also shows live ambient pressure in millibars beside its sensor status when the phone provides a barometer reading.

Why
Put here the reading you check often, so one glance is enough.
Next
Tapping the card copies the coordinates to the clipboard when it shows Coordinates and there is a position, and opens the counter when it shows Counter; on every other tile a tap does nothing.
Undo
A tap changes no data in the app. The pick is not remembered: the dashboard opens with Speed each time.
Tip

On Stopwatch the card has its own buttons: Start (it becomes Stop while the time runs), Reset and Open stopwatch. The first two time one person; the last opens the full stopwatch for several.

2ResetStarts the trip, or the step count, over.

Appears only when the big card shows a tile of the trip: Speed, Trip Distance, Time on trip, Moving time, Ascent, Average speed or Steps. On Steps it resets only the step count; on the others it resets the whole trip: distance, time on trip and moving time, ascent and descent, average and maximum speed.

Why
Use it at the start of a new leg, so the figures describe only that leg.
Next
The trip figures go back to zero and count again from the next reading; the step count starts from zero when the phone next reports a step.
Undo
No. It asks for no confirmation and the reset cannot be undone.
3The grid of tilesAll the other readings, one tile each.

A grid of smaller tiles, two to a row, one for each reading that is not on the big card: for example altitude, compass, coordinates, satellites, steps, the trip figures, sun times and weather. Tapping a tile moves it to the big card.

Why
Everything the dashboard can read sits on one scrolling page, so no menu is needed to find a reading.
Next
The tapped tile takes the place of the big card and the old one returns to the grid.
Undo
Nothing is changed or lost; tap the previous tile to bring it back.
Tip

Tiles whose sensor the phone does not have are left out of the grid. The weather tiles - for example Weather, Wind and Humidity - need internet and a position: the app sends your coordinates to the Open-Meteo service for them.

4MapShows you on a map.

Hands your position to a maps app on the phone; if no app can open it, a web maps link opens in a browser. Both leave this app, and until a position is found the maps app opens without your point.

Why
Use it to see where you are on a map.
Next
The maps app (or the browser) opens in front of this app, which stays open behind it.
Undo
Opening a map changes nothing in this app.
5SharePasses your position on as text.

Puts your coordinates and a web maps link into the system share sheet, where you choose where they go. Until a position is found the button only shows a short message and shares nothing.

Why
Use it to tell someone where you are.
Next
Choose a recipient in the share sheet: the app itself sends nothing.
Undo
Back closes the sheet and nothing leaves; what you already sent from another app cannot be taken back from here.
6Reset TripStarts the trip over from zero.

Resets the whole trip, not only its distance - time on trip and moving time, ascent and descent, average and maximum speed - whatever the big card shows. The one exception is Steps: when the big card shows it, this button resets only the step count.

Why
Press it when you set off on a new leg, so the figures count only that leg.
Next
The trip figures go back to zero and count again from the next reading; the step count starts from zero when the phone next reports a step.
Undo
No. It asks for no confirmation and the reset cannot be undone.
Tip

The trip is kept between visits and after the app restarts, and it grows only while the dashboard is open. This button and the reset on the big card are the ways to start it over.

7SOS signalOpens the SOS screen.

Opens the SOS screen. The signal starts as soon as that screen opens: a siren and a strobe repeat the Morse SOS pattern, and a paired watch signals too when it is in range. It sounds and blinks; it does not call anyone and sends no message.

Why
Use it when you need to be noticed. It is on the dashboard so the signal is one tap away, without going back to the start screen.
Next
The signal keeps going until you press Stop signal or leave that screen with Back; the SOS emergency signal page explains it.
Undo
Stop signal ends the signal at once.
8Help (F1)Opens this page.

The help button in the top-right corner of the top bar opens this page without leaving the app.

Why
It explains the dashboard with a picture, without a network.
Undo
Opening it changes nothing; Back closes it.
Shortcut
F1 on a connected keyboard does the same.
9Permission required Shown only when neededAsks for access the dashboard is missing.

A card at the top of the screen that appears only when an access is missing: location first, then physical activity for the step count (only on phones that count steps). It says what stays unavailable and offers Allow; when the system no longer shows its request, the button reads Open settings.

Why
Without location the dashboard cannot show speed, altitude, coordinates and sun times, and without physical activity it cannot count steps; the card names which one is missing.
Next
Allow opens the system permission dialog; Open settings opens the app's page in the system settings, which leaves this app. Once the access is granted and you are back on the dashboard, the card disappears.
Undo
You can withdraw the access later in the system settings; the card then returns.
Tip

The dashboard needs precise location: if you allow only approximate location, the card stays. Location is read only while this screen is open, never in the background.
