Your first walk

  1. Open Tourist. The start screen shows four programs: Tourist dashboard, Lights, SOS emergency signal and Stopwatch. Back on any program returns to it.
  2. Open Tourist dashboard. The first time it asks for location access: press Allow. Without it the dashboard cannot read your position or steps. Tourist reads the location only while a Tourist screen is open.
  3. Read the big card, which shows Speed at first, and tap a tile in the grid to read it large.
  4. At the start of every new leg press Reset Trip, so the trip counts from zero.

Help is built into every screen of the phone app: the question mark in the top bar explains the screen you are on, offline.

Read the dashboard

The dashboard shows what your phone reads on the trail: one reading large on the big card, the others as tiles below it.

  • Compass: the big card draws a compass rose that turns as you turn the phone, with the heading in degrees, the cardinal direction and the accuracy of the sensor.
  • Coordinates: tap the big card while it shows Coordinates to copy the position.
  • Altitude, satellites, sunrise and sunset, steps, trip distance and the weather at your position are tiles of their own. For the weather the coordinates of your position go to the weather service Open-Meteo, the one request that leaves the phone on its own.
  • Map shows you in a maps app and Share passes the position on as text. Both leave Tourist.
The point

Speed, altitude, heading and coordinates are readings of the phone sensors and of satellite positioning. They are not a certified instrument.

Send the SOS signal

  1. Open SOS emergency signal only when you want the signal: it starts as soon as the screen opens, a siren and a flashing light repeating the Morse SOS pattern.
  2. Choose what it uses: Sound and light, Sound only or Light only. The running signal changes at once and the choice is kept.
  3. A paired watch signals too. You can lock the phone or open another app: the signal goes on.
  4. Press Stop signal or leave the screen with Back. The phone and the paired watch stop. If you do nothing, the phone ends its own signal after 30 minutes.
The point

SOS sounds, blinks and reaches your paired watch. It sends no SMS, makes no call and contacts no one, and it does not replace calling the emergency services.

Use the water flashlight

  1. Open Lights and choose Water flashlight. Android asks you to pin the app: confirm it before you use the light with wet hands.
  2. The camera flash and the white screen come on together, and the screen shows the time and a hint that it is locked. Touches and Back do not close the lamp.
  3. To put it out press a volume key. The light goes out and you are back where you came from.

The home screen widget Water flashlight opens it in one tap.

The point

A touch lock, not waterproofing: it keeps raindrops and wet gloves from pressing buttons. It does not protect the phone from water.

Use the screen and camera lights

  • Front flashlight: the screen as a lamp, in a colour you choose with Light color. Drag up or down to change the brightness. A tap outside the controls closes it.
  • Camera flashlight: tap the row to switch the camera flash on and tap it again to switch it off. No screen opens.
  • A checkbox on the Lights screen keeps the camera flashlight one tap away in the notification shade. On Android 13 and later the app first asks for permission to show notifications.

Time a group with the stopwatch

  1. Open Stopwatch. If you time more than one person, open the settings first and choose two or four Participants.
  2. The count starts for everyone as soon as the screen opens. To start from zero press Reset all, then Start each participant in their own area, or everyone at once with Start all.
  3. Press Lap in a participant's area to mark a lap without stopping, and Stop when they finish. Stop all stops everyone together.
  4. Press Result to turn the times into text you can save or share.
The point

The times are not kept: leaving the screen with Back clears them. The stopwatch is for everyday timing, not an official timing device.

On the watch

The Tourist watch app for Wear OS brings the Water flashlight, SOS and the Stopwatch to your wrist, with or without the phone nearby. SOS started on the phone starts on the paired watch, and the other way round. The Tourist watch face is a separate package.

Settings and languages

Settings holds the theme, the language, the units, the screen-awake setting and About. Changes apply at once. The interface is available in English, Russian and Ukrainian.

What Tourist asks for

  • Location, only while a Tourist screen is open, for the dashboard.
  • Camera, only to switch the camera flash on and off. Tourist takes no pictures.
  • Notifications, only for the shortcuts you switch on and the Stop button of the signal.

The complete list, and what stays on your devices, is in the privacy policy.
